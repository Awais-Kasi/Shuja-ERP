# Shuja ERP — Chhoti Urdu Video Scripts (Client Training)

Yeh 4 chhoti videos (har ek ~1–1.5 minute) client ko software seekhne mein sab se zyada madad dengi.
Apne phone ya computer se **screen record** karein, neeche likhi **Urdu narration** bol kar sunayein,
aur client ko WhatsApp par bhej dein. Aaraam se, dheemi awaaz mein bolein.

> Tip: Recording se pehle app ko **"Aasaan" mode** par rakhein (sidebar ke neeche) taake sirf zaroori
> cheezein nazar aayein. Pehli dafa login par jo "Salaam" card aata hai, woh bhi dikha dein.

---

## 🎬 Video 1 — "Nai Bikri" (Maal bechna) · ~75 second

**Kya dikhana hai (screen):** Dashboard → "Nai Bikri" button → bikri form bharna → "Bikri pakki karein".

**Narration (bolें):**
> "Assalam-o-alaikum. Aaj seekhte hain maal kaise bechna hai — bohat aasaan hai.
> Ghar (dashboard) par yeh bada hara button hai, **Nai Bikri**. Ispe click karein.
> Sab se pehle **customer** chunein — agar naam zaroori nahi to 'Cash' rehne dein.
> Phir **maal** chunein aur likhein kitna becha. Dekhein, **rate khud** aa gaya — zaroorat ho to badal lein.
> Agar customer ne **abhi paisa de diya** hai to 'Paisa mila' mein woh raqam likhein; agar udhaar hai to khaali chhoR dein.
> Bas, neeche **'Bikri pakki karein'** dabayein — ho gayi bikri! Khata khud update ho jayega."

---

## 🎬 Video 2 — "Nai Khareed" (Maal khareedna) · ~70 second

**Kya dikhana hai:** Dashboard → "Nai Khareed" → supplier + maal + rate → "Khareed pakki karein".

**Narration:**
> "Ab seekhte hain maal **khareedna**. Ghar par neela button hai, **Nai Khareed** — ispe click karein.
> **Supplier** chunein, jis se aap ne maal liya. Phir **maal** chunein, kitna aaya aur kya **rate** tha woh likhein.
> Agar aap ne **paisa de diya** hai to 'Paisa diya' mein likhein; udhaar hai to khaali rehne dein.
> **'Khareed pakki karein'** dabayein. Supplier ka khata khud ban jayega — aap ko kitna dena hai, saaf nazar aayega."

---

## 🎬 Video 3 — "Udhaar — kis se lena, kis ko dena" · ~60 second

**Kya dikhana hai:** Menu → "Udhaar" → baayein (lena) / daayein (dena) → WhatsApp button.

**Narration:**
> "Yeh sab se kaam ki cheez hai — **Udhaar**. Menu se 'Udhaar' kholें.
> **Baayein taraf** har woh customer hai jis se aap ne **lena** hai — saamne raqam likhi hai.
> **Daayein taraf** har woh supplier jise aap ne **dena** hai.
> Kisi ke naam ke aage **WhatsApp** ka nishan hai — ispe daba kar aap seedha usay yaad-dehani bhej sakte hain.
> Ab kabhi bhoolenge nahi ke kis se kitna lena-dena hai."

---

## 🎬 Video 4 — "Aaj ka Hisaab, Dhoondo aur Madad" · ~80 second

**Kya dikhana hai:** Menu → "Aaj ka Hisaab" → upar "Dhoondo" search → header ka "❓ Madad" button.

**Narration:**
> "Aakhri video — teen chhoti magar bohat kaam ki cheezein.
> Ek: **Aaj ka Hisaab**. Menu se kholें — yahan ek nazar mein aaj kitni bikri hui, kitna kharcha, aur kitna cash aaya-gaya.
> Do: upar **Dhoondo** ka khaana hai. Kisi bhi customer, maal ya bill ka naam likhें — seedha us ki screen khul jayegi. Lambi list mein dhoondne ki zaroorat nahi.
> Teen: aur sab se zaroori — har screen par upar **Madad** ka button hai. Jahan samajh na aaye, ise dabayein — usi page ki aasaan Urdu madad aa jayegi. Wahin se yeh **chhapne wala guide** bhi khul jata hai jo aap computer ke paas laga sakte hain.
> Bas itna hi! In chaar kaamon se aap ka rozana ka kaam ho jayega. Shukriya."

---

### Record karne ke baad
- Chaaron videos client ke WhatsApp par bhej dें (ya ek group bana den).
- App ke andar **❓ Madad → "Poora Guide (chaapein)"** se ek safha print kar ke computer ke paas chipka dें.
- Pehle hafte client se rozana poochें ke kahin phansa to nahi — chhoti jhijhak jald door ho jayegi.
